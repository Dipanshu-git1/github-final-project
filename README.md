# Simple Interest Calculator

This project calculates simple interest from the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal** is the initial amount of money.
- **Rate** is the annual rate of interest, expressed as a percentage.
- **Time** is the duration in years.

## Run the calculator

Make the script executable and run it in Bash:

```bash
chmod +x simple-interest.sh
./simple-interest.sh
```

Enter the principal, annual rate of interest, and time period when prompted. The calculator displays the simple interest and total amount (principal plus interest).

## Example

For a principal of 1000, a rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

- Simple interest: 100
- Total amount: 1100
